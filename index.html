Understanding reading difficulties

Why do some students find
reading hard?

Reading is complex — and struggling with it is far more common than most people realise.

01

What is a reading difficulty?

A reading difficulty means a student finds it harder than expected to decode words, maintain focus, or recall what they've read. It's a difference in how the brain processes text — not a measure of intelligence.

Affects 20% of students
02

Why do students struggle?

Factors include visual stress from bright pages, difficulty tracking lines, slow reading pace, and limited working memory. Most classrooms simply can't provide the individual adjustments each student truly needs.

Environmental & cognitive
03

How technology helps

Text-to-speech bridges hearing and reading. Adjustable fonts and colours reduce eye strain. Focus mode cuts distractions. Together these give every student a genuine, fair chance to engage with any text.
